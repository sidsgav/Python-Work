What is Komodo Hub?

Komodo Hub stands at the heart of a unique framework that is committed to supporting schools, communities, and conservationist in their goal to protect endangered species of Indonesia. Through the utilisation of technology and equipment whilst running education schooling and other interactive sessions in creative engagements, Komodo Hub is here to ensure that when it comes to saving the environment, we can all collectively do something about it. The platform also communicates scientific findings to the world and it is clouded with all the necessary means in the form of digital content for learning conservation, initiative for conservation among other engaging resources in helping people to appreciate biodiversity. Quite notably, users such as students, teachers and the general public can analyse web based conservation data, get engaged in citizen science projects or even assist in biodiversity preservation by reporting to sightings of specific species during suitable periods or getting involved in mutual conservation schemes where animals are concerned. In an attempt to introduce some sense of responsibility and engagement, Komodo Hub promotes behavioral change, which is taking any positive steps aimed at protecting some most preserved ecosystems in Indonesia.
