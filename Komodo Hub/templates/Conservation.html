What is Animal Conservation?

Protecting endangered animals is crucial for maintaining biodiversity and ecosystem balance. You can help by supporting wildlife conservation organizations, adopting sustainable habits, and avoiding products that contribute to habitat destruction. Advocate for stronger laws against poaching and illegal wildlife trade, and spread awareness about the importance of protecting threatened species. Volunteering at wildlife sanctuaries or donating to conservation programs also makes a big difference. Every action counts in ensuring a future for endangered animals.
